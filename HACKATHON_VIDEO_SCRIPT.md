# LivingPlus — 3-Minute Demo Script (Meta AI Hackathon)

About 350 words. All demo, no slides. Read at a natural, unhurried pace for 3:00.

How to read this file:

- **SAY** = read out loud.
- **SHOW** = what is on screen. Never read these.
- `(...)` = pause for one second.

## Timing at a glance

| Time | Demo beat | Show |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Hook | Title card |
| 0:15 | Guest finds a bed | OTP login, live bed list |
| 0:45 | Owner approves | One-click approval |
| 1:05 | Resident portal | Stay, rent, food, repairs |
| 1:20 | Leak report | One sentence + photo, ticket filed |
| 1:55 | Status check | Agent answers from records |
| 2:10 | Dues question | Ledger-backed answer |
| 2:30 | Safety | Confirmation prompt, audit log |
| 2:45 | Close | Closing card |

---

## 0:00 — Hook

SHOW: title card "LivingPlus".

SAY:

> Hello everyone. I am [your name], and this is LivingPlus.
> Finding a PG, running a PG, living in a PG — watch it all happen live. (...)

## 0:15 — Guest finds a bed

SHOW: guest OTP login, room list with live availability.

SAY:

> A guest signs in with just a phone number.
> No password, no form.
> They see real bed-level availability.
> Not photos from five years ago — live beds, live rents.
> And they request a bed in plain words.

## 0:45 — Owner approves

SHOW: owner approves booking in one click.

SAY:

> The owner approves in one click.
> In the workspace, or by one sentence to the agent.
> Done. That phone number now opens a resident portal.

## 1:05 — Resident portal

SHOW: resident portal — stay, rent, food, repairs in one place.

SAY:

> Stay, rent, food, repairs — one place.
> This is the resident's whole PG life from here on.

## 1:20 — Leak report

SHOW: resident reports leaking tap in one sentence with photo. Ticket appears with category, room, bed, image. Owner receives it.

SAY:

> Now watch this.
> The resident reports a leaking tap in one sentence, with a photo.
> The agent files a full ticket.
> Category, room, bed, image attached.
> The owner gets it instantly.
> No call, no chasing, no notebook.

## 1:55 — Status check

SHOW: resident asks the agent for the ticket status. Answer appears.

SAY:

> Later the resident asks — what happened to my complaint?
> The answer comes straight from the records.
> No follow-up calls needed.

## 2:10 — Dues question

SHOW: owner asks this month's outstanding. Ledger summary appears.

SAY:

> Meanwhile the owner just asks for this month's outstanding.
> And gets a clean ledger-backed answer, by property and month.
> No spreadsheets.

## 2:30 — Safety

SHOW: confirmation prompt, audit log.

SAY:

> And it is safe to operate.
> Big actions pause for human confirmation.
> Privacy is enforced in code.
> Everything is audit-logged.

## 2:45 — Close

SHOW: closing card.

SAY:

> Residents get a smooth stay.
> Owners get a calm operation.
> Through chat or clicks, powered by Meta Spark underneath.
> Thank you. I am [your name], this was LivingPlus. (...)
