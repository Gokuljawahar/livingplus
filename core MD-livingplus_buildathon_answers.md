## Q1. Provide one sentence summary of your new product/project that you are planning to build. *(text, max 1024 chars)*

LivingPlus is an end-to-end digitized living platform for urban migration: students and young professionals moving to a new city can discover verified stays, match bed-by-bed with compatible roommates via VibeCheck, complete digital KYC and booking, order food and cleaning, pay bills like electricity transparently, log photo or voice complaints, and move out with documented deposit settlements, while independent PG and apartment owners run their properties with modular tools and AI assistance, switching on only the services they need, backed by WhatsApp-native communication and everyday service updates.

## Q2. What is the main problem that you are solving with the new product idea? *(text, max 1024 chars)*

Moving to a new city for study or work is stressful, slow, and untrustworthy. Discovery relies on scattered references and outdated listings, while onboarding, KYC paperwork, and advance payments lack transparency, causing newcomers to search for weeks and still choose poorly. Daily living is largely unorganized: food, cleaning, rent receipts, complaints, dues, and deposits are tracked via notebooks and ad-hoc messages, leading to declining service quality and move-out disputes. Supply is fragmented: most urban rentals are run by small independent owners lacking unified digital systems, while branded operators only manage their own inventory. Roommates are allotted without considering sleep, cleanliness, or noise habits, triggering friction and early exits. The result is a fragmented resident journey, low mutual trust, and owners competing on rent cuts rather than service quality.

## Q3. Who would be a typical user/customer? *(text, max 1024 chars)*

The primary users are young people relocating to a new city (students, coaching aspirants, and early-career professionals aged 18 to 30) alongside parents who often fund and vet the stay. The paying customers are independent apartment and PG owners, ranging from single-property hosts to small multi-property operators seeking higher occupancy, calmer operations, and ancillary service revenue. Day-to-day operational users include wardens, cooks, housekeeping staff, and accountants, each given role-based digital access. Local food, cleaning, and mobility providers act as service partners, tapping into concentrated demand within each property. Commercially, residents and staff use the platform free of cost, owners pay for modular management tools and visibility, and partners share revenue, aligning growth with owner onboarding and dense property-level resident communities.

## Q4. What is your solution to this problem? *(text, max 1024 chars)*

LivingPlus digitizes the entire urban stay lifecycle in three pillars: (1) Verified discovery near campuses and offices; (2) A unified resident journey featuring explainable VibeCheck roommate matching against live beds (with a post-move-in rematch window), seamless digital KYC and secure booking, guided onboarding, flexible food and cleaning orders, itemized utility billing with receipts, photo/voice issue tracking, and timestamped move-in/out condition records with transparent deposit ledgers; and (3) A modular owner console where independent hosts manage properties as private workspaces, toggling services on or off with role-based staff access. AI assists both sides with human approval for financial records, while residents access core updates, reminders, and quick actions natively over WhatsApp.

## Q6. What is the potential business model of this product? *(text, max 1024 chars)*

The business model combines three complementary revenue streams. First, a SaaS subscription for property owners priced per property or per bed, paired with modular add-ons (such as VibeCheck, digital KYC verification, meal management, or smart utility integrations) so owners pay only for active features and expand usage over time. Second, transaction revenue through commissions on confirmed digital bookings and optional promoted listings that help owners fill vacancies faster. Third, a revenue share from everyday living services booked through the platform (including meals, housekeeping, and local mobility), where concentrated resident demand makes customer acquisition attractive for local partners. Keeping the resident and staff experience free accelerates adoption, increasing property-level volume that drives partner value and supports accessible owner pricing and long-term retention.

## Q7. Who will be your primary competitor(s) and how is your solution different from them? *(text, max 1024 chars)*

Competition falls into three buckets: (1) Basic PG management tools (StayPe, Domvio, Homly, PG Saathi, VAASYO) that focus narrowly on rent collection and chat-based reminders; (2) Branded co-living operators (Stanza, Zolo, Colive) that deliver a cohesive resident experience, but strictly within their own leased inventory; and (3) Listing portals (NoBroker and peers) that handle discovery and agreements but disengage after move-in. LivingPlus differentiates by uniting the complete lifecycle (verified discovery, bed-level VibeCheck roommate matching with rematching, digital KYC and booking, daily services, issue resolution, and transparent move-outs) into one connected digital platform for independent owners. WhatsApp serves as our seamless communication and service layer rather than the entire product, creating lasting defensibility through structured property records and partner networks.

## Q8. What will be your primary and secondary target countries, if applicable? *(text, max 1024 chars)*

The primary target market is India, home to massive student and workforce migration, a rapidly expanding paying-guest and shared-rental sector, and a supply landscape dominated by independent small-scale owners, the exact fragmentation LivingPlus is built to structure. Initial rollout will target dense education and employment hubs, scaling through owner clusters around university campuses and tech corridors. Longer-term secondary markets include regions with high migrant populations and fragmented shared-housing supply, such as the UAE and Southeast Asian hubs like the Philippines and Indonesia. Because the core architecture (digitized resident lifecycle, modular owner workspace, and AI copilot) is universal, international expansion requires only localizing languages, digital KYC norms, payment rails, and service partners rather than rebuilding the platform.

## Q10. How are you planning to use AI for this solution? *(text, max 1024 chars)*

AI operates as the intelligence layer across the entire resident and owner lifecycle. Three flagship capabilities anchor this: (1) VibeCheck: an 8 to 10 question lifestyle compatibility engine (focusing on daily habits like sleep schedules, cleanliness, noise levels, guests, and kitchen routines rather than personal background) scored against available beds with transparent match reasons and a post-move-in rematch option to reduce early exits; (2) Digital KYC & onboarding automation: AI extracts, verifies, and flags discrepancies in identity documents to streamline compliant check-ins; and (3) Multimodal & WhatsApp-native assistance: residents can submit photo or voice complaints, query dues in plain language, and trigger food or cleaning requests seamlessly. For owners, an AI copilot summarizes occupancy, dues, and tickets, drafts notices, and coordinates staff, bounded by strict per-property isolation and human approvals.

## Q12. Please describe how are you planning to leverage the above selected Google's AI models to tackle the problem you mentioned? *(text, max 1024 chars)*

We plan to leverage the Gemini API as the core reasoning and tool-calling engine powering discovery, VibeCheck roommate scoring, digital KYC document verification, booking workflows, billing queries, maintenance triage, service orders, and staff task routing, strictly scoped by property and user role. Gemini's multimodal vision will verify ID proofs during onboarding and classify photo complaints (such as plumbing leaks or appliance faults) into structured tickets, while structured outputs keep VibeCheck transparent with clear match reasons. Gemini Live will enable low-latency, local-language voice interactions with natural interruption, helping residents who prefer speaking over typing and giving owners hands-free operational briefings. Prompts and guardrails will be prototyped and evaluated in Google AI Studio against role-isolation test cases, ensuring residents never access peer finances or KYC records.

## Q14. How do you intend to deploy and scale your product on Google Cloud? *(text, max 1024 chars)*

LivingPlus will be deployed on managed Google Cloud services to start lean and scale elastically without re-architecture. Cloud Run will host our core backend APIs, AI orchestration, and WhatsApp webhook services, scaling automatically during peak college admission and corporate hiring cycles and scaling to zero when idle. Firebase Hosting will deliver the web platform and modular owner console globally with low latency. Cloud SQL (managed PostgreSQL) will store multi-tenant relational data with automated backups and strict logical isolation per property. Cloud Storage will secure listing media, VibeCheck assets, and encrypted digital KYC documents via time-limited signed URLs. Google Maps Platform will power location-aware discovery near campuses and offices, while Cloud Logging and Monitoring track system health and messaging compliance as properties onboard.
