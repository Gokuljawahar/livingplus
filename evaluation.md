The speaker is explaining the SPARK framework—an evaluation and pitching model likely used for a hackathon or innovation challenge. Here is the full breakdown and transcription of what is being discussed:
S - Significance
 * Core Meaning: The significance of the idea.
 * Details: It depends on what problem is actually being solved. The idea must be valuable and "worth enough to be pursued".
P - Potential
 * Core Meaning: The paying potential of the idea.
 * Details: Whether the idea is viable enough to be sold in the market. It evaluates if there is an available consumer base and sufficient market potential to sell the solution.
A - AI Relevance
 * Core Meaning: The relevance of Artificial Intelligence to the idea.
 * Details: How genuinely relevant AI is to solving the problem, rather than "merely having AI for the sake of it".
R - Realistic
 * Core Meaning: How realistic the idea is.
 * Details: Given real-world constraints, the idea should not be a "fairy-tale one". It must align with practical, real-world limitations.
K - Key Thing
 * Core Meaning: The key takeaway or core feature to showcase.
 * Details: Since everything cannot be built in a single day of a hackathon, teams must choose their "K" wisely. It is about identifying the most important element to put forward so the entire idea is communicated "loud and clear" to the judging panel.
