The Significance

The four moments where a PG stay breaks

Every year, lakhs of newcomers relocate to tech and college hubs like Chennai into fragmented rentals.

01 · Discovery
WhatsApp Forwards
Adversarial hunt through brokers, unverified listings, hidden utility add-ons, and upfront advance fraud.
02 · Living
Notebook Operations
Meals, cleaning schedules, rent receipts, and complaints tracked across paper ledgers and chaotic WhatsApp chats.
03 · Roommates
4 AM Friction
Blind bed allotment. Sleep, tidiness, and noise mismatches cause early tenant departures and lost revenue.
04 · Move-Out
Deposit Disputes
Subjective damage claims with zero move-in baseline documentation, causing hostile departures and distrust.

Business Model & Monetization

Three complementary revenue streams

Residents and operational staff use LivingPlus for free. Independent owners pay for value.

Stream 1 · Recurring
Per-Bed SaaS
Tiered monthly fee per bed for the owner console, modular staff seats, automated bookkeeping, and PG pulse intelligence.
Stream 2 · Transaction
Booking Commissions
Commission fee on confirmed digital bookings, paired with optional promoted listings that fill vacancies 40% faster.
Stream 3 · Services
Living Services Share
Revenue share on meals, housekeeping, laundry, and local mobility booked by dense resident communities.
